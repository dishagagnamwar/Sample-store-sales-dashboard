# 📊 Sample Store US Sales Analysis – Tableau Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Tableau dashboard** created using the **Sample Store US dataset**. The purpose of this project is to analyze sales performance, profitability, customers, products, categories, and regional trends across the United States.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful visual insights that can help understand business performance and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
* Identify sales trends over time.
* Compare performance across different regions and states.
* Analyze product and category performance.
* Identify top-performing and underperforming products.
* Understand customer and segment performance.
* Analyze profitability across different regions.
* Present insights through an interactive and easy-to-understand dashboard.

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Sales & Profit Analysis

The dashboard provides an overview of sales and profit performance, helping identify how the business is performing overall and how profitability changes across different periods and categories.

### 2. Regional Analysis

Sales and profit are analyzed across different regions and states in the US. This helps identify high-performing and low-performing geographical areas.

### 3. Product Analysis

Products are analyzed based on sales, profit, and overall performance. This makes it easier to identify products that contribute significantly to business revenue and profitability.

### 4. Category Analysis

Different product categories are compared to understand their contribution to sales and profit and identify categories that perform better than others.

### 5. Customer & Segment Analysis

Customer segments are analyzed to understand their purchasing contribution and profitability. This helps provide a better understanding of customer behavior and business performance.

### 6. Time-Based Analysis

Sales and profit trends are examined over time to identify changes in business performance and understand seasonal or monthly patterns.
